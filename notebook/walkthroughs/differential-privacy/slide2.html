Effect of Privacy Loss Parameter on Results: Walkthrough¶

Set the epsilon value to 0.5 to see the effect of reducing privacy loss (and thus also reducing the accuracy of the results). In this case, we can see that the blue and orange vertical bars can differ significantly. This means that the campaign totals for any individual campaign may be particularly noisy in a differentially private result.
